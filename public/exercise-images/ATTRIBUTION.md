## Exercise library data and photos

Names, muscles, equipment, instructions and the two-frame demonstration photos for the extended library come from free-exercise-db (https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db), released into the public domain under the Unlicense. The data is generated into `lib/exercise-library.json` by `scripts/build-exercise-library.mjs`, pinned to one commit; photos load from that commit on jsDelivr. No Gym Visual / ExerciseDB media is used: those animations require a separate licence from Gym Visual.
